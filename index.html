How it works

Know before it bites.

Glacis feels the cold for you. It keeps watch, reads the trend, and speaks up while there is still time to act.

Sense 1 of 3

It keeps watch on your hands.

Quietly, the whole time you are out. You keep your mind on the climb, the route or the job.

Read 2 of 3

It reads the trend, not one number.

How fast you are cooling matters more than where you are right now, so Glacis weighs the change against the wind and the air around you.

Warn 3 of 3

It speaks up early, and calmly.

A vibration you can feel through a mitt, a small light, and one plain sentence. Early enough to warm up and keep going.
